Last Updated: September 25, 2026

Request deletion of your Tahet account

You can request deletion of your Tahet account and associated personal data without installing or signing into the app.

Email support@tahet.app from the email address associated with your Tahet account, with the subject Delete my Tahet account. If you cannot access that email address, contact support for help verifying ownership. Do not send your password.

The request covers your account information, profile, and associated personal data. Personal information is deleted within 30 days, except information that must be retained for legal purposes, as described in the Data Retention section below.

You can also initiate account deletion from Settings in the Tahet app.

Welcome to Tahet ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we handle your information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and display name when you create an account
  • Profile Information: Profile photo (optional) that you choose to upload
  • Game Data: Game results, scores, Elo ratings, achievements, and statistics
  • Group Information: Groups you create or join, and your membership roles
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version for crash reporting and performance optimization
  • Notifications and Diagnostics: Push tokens and installation identifiers, notification preferences, crash logs, diagnostic information and app-session activity. Network requests also expose an IP address to the services receiving them.
  • Content and Safety: Club names, descriptions, match notes and other content you submit; reports of inappropriate content, the reported content, block preferences, acceptance of community rules and moderation decisions. Chat messages are processed on versions that offer chat; the Android launch does not offer chat.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Tahet app and its features
  • Calculate and display Elo ratings and leaderboards
  • Track achievements and game statistics
  • Send notifications about game invites, results, and achievements (with your permission)
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs through crash reporting
  • Respond to support requests
  • Review reports, enforce community rules, honor blocks and prevent abuse

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in these circumstances:

  • Other Users: Your display name, profile photo, and public player statistics can be visible to other signed-in users. Club content and match records are shown to participants and members according to the feature. Your real name is displayed only when you enable that option. Reports and block preferences are not published to other users.
  • Service Providers: Clerk provides authentication, Sentry processes crashes and diagnostics, Railway hosts our backend and database, UploadThing hosts uploaded images, and Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver Android notifications. These services receive the data needed to provide their functions on our behalf.
  • Moderation: Authorized Tahet moderators can review report details and a copy of the reported content. Contact support@tahet.app for follow-up or an appeal.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure authentication via Clerk
  • Secure token storage on your device

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

Limited safety records, including removed content needed to prevent its reappearance and records needed to handle reports or appeals, may be retained for abuse prevention or legal obligations. Contact support@tahet.app to request review and deletion of these records. Shared match records may remain where they no longer identify you.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of push notifications

7. Children's Privacy

Tahet is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or via email.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at:

Email: support@tahet.app
